## Stream 1: Qordi SaaS ($50K/year target)

### Model
- **Free Tier:** Basic ingest (1 idea/day), limited guidance
- **Pro Tier ($9/mo):** Unlimited ingest, daily guidance, asset generation

### Pricing Rationale
- $9/mo = $108/year (easy to commit to)
- Low enough for solopreneurs
- High enough to signal quality
- Room to upsell (Teams tier later)

### Metrics
- **Users:** 500 (Year 1 goal)
- **Conversion (Free → Pro):** 20% = 100 paying users
- **MRR:** 100 users × $9 = $900/mo
- **Annual:** $900 × 12 = $10,800

**To reach $50K/year:**
- Need: 460 paying users
- Or: 20% conversion from 2,300 free users
- Or: 38/mo growth from month 6 onward

### Growth Levers
1. **Organic:** Word-of-mouth, ProductHunt, Reddit
2. **Content:** Blog posts, email marketing
3. **Community:** Discord, early user group
4. **Partnerships:** AI tool affiliates, productivity communities

### CAC/LTV Analysis
- **CAC:** $20 (content + community, no paid ads)
- **LTV:** $108 (100 users × $9 × 12 months ÷ 100)
- **Payback:** 2.4 months (acceptable)
- **LTV:CAC ratio:** 5.4:1 (healthy)

### Churn Management
- Target churn: <5%/month
- Strategies:
  - Daily nudges keep users engaged
  - Weekly feature updates
  - Monthly "wins" email (celebrate progress)
  - NPS surveys (remove friction)

## Stream 3: Merch Store ($20K/year target)

### Model
- **Print-on-demand:** Printful integration (0 upfront cost)
- **Designs:** Based on trends (tie to OpenClaw trends)
- **Channels:** werii.com store, Etsy, social links

### Mechanics
1. Trend Analysis identifies hot topic (e.g., "AI Ethics")
2. Commission design (Midjourney or designer)
3. Upload to Printful
4. Schedule X post: "New merch drop: [Design]"
5. Users purchase
6. Printful fulfills + ships
7. Revenue: ~20% per order (after production + shipping)

### Unit Economics
- **Avg order value:** $40 (t-shirt $25, hoodie $50)
- **Profit margin:** $8-10 per order (20-25%)
- **Orders/month target:** 50 orders = $400-500/mo profit
- **Annual:** $5,000-6,000

**To reach $20K/year:**
- Need: 200+ orders/mo
- Or: Higher margins (premium positioning)
- Or: Bundle deals (merch + digital product)

### Design Strategy
- Launch 1 design every 2 weeks
- Restock bestsellers
- Limited edition (drive urgency)
- Tie to Qordi milestones ("First 1K users" hoodie)

### Promotion
- Tag in social posts
- Link from blog
- Email to list ("New design out")
- Affiliate program (creators get 10% commission)

## Cost Structure

### Fixed Monthly Costs
- Hotinger VPS: $10
- Supabase: $25 (estimated, scales with data)
- OpenAI API: $50 (usage-based)
- Stripe: 2.9% + $0.30 per transaction
- Email (SendGrid): $20
- **Total: ~$155/mo = $1,860/year**

### Variable Costs (per transaction)
- Stripe fees: 2.9% + $0.30
- Printful: 60% COGS (profit margin 20-25%)
- SaaS apps: Negligible (already paying OpenAI)

### Profit Margins by Stream
| Stream | Revenue | Costs | Margin |
|--------|---------|-------|--------|
| Qordi SaaS | 100% | 15% (API) | 85% |
| Email/Sponsorship | 100% | 0% | 100% |
| Merch | 100% | 75% (COGS + fees) | 25% |
| Apps | 100% | 20% (API + infra) | 80% |

## Contingency Plans

### If Qordi doesn't gain traction
- Offer to custom-build for enterprise (higher margins)
- Pivot to B2B (teams, not solopreneurs)
- Free tier + premium support tier

### If merch doesn't sell
- Stop designing, focus on core product
- Or: Partner with print shop for bulk orders (lower COGS)

### If no sponsorship interest
- Focus on affiliate revenue instead
- Guest post on high-traffic blogs (build SEO)
- Create digital products (courses, templates)

### If apps don't convert
- Build fewer, better apps (focus over spread)
- Double down on Qordi instead
- Consider agency model (custom work)
